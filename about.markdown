## The Origin Story

Beer Club started the way most good things do: someone said "we should hang out more" and then actually did something about it.

A few friends, tired of the endless "let's grab a drink sometime" texts that never went anywhere, decided to just pick a day. Same time. Every month. No excuses.

## What It's Become

It's not really about beer (see Rule 4). It's about protecting time for the people who matter. Jobs get busy. Kids happen. Life fills every gap you give it.

Beer Club is just a name for the thing that says "this time is spoken for."

## Why This Site?

We figured if it worked for us, maybe it'd work for others. So here it is - permission to start your own. Steal the rules, ignore the rules, make up new ones. The only thing that matters is showing up.

## Start Your Own

Seriously, do it. Text three friends right now. Pick a day. Put it on the calendar. Repeat until you're old.

That's it. That's the whole secret.
